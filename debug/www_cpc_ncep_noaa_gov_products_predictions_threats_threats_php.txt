          <h2>Valid Thursday November 19, 2026 to Wednesday November 25, 2026</h2>US Hazards Outlook<br>NWS Climate Prediction Center College Park MD<br>300 PM 
EST November 11 2026<br><br><u>Synopsis</u>: Mid-level low pressure is forecast 
to extend from the Aleutians southeastward to the West Coast during mid to late 
November. An active pattern is likely across the southern and central 
Contiguous U.S. (CONUS) as multiple low pressure systems track eastward from 
Nov 19-25. The enhanced Pacific flow favors above-normal temperatures for a 
majority of the CONUS with no sign of any early season outbreaks of anomalous 
cold. <br><br><u>Hazards</u> <ul><li>Moderate risk of heavy precipitation for 
eastern portions of the Southern Plains and the Lower Mississippi Valley, 
Fri-Sat, Nov 20-21.<li>Slight risk of heavy precipitation for the Southern and Central Plains, 
Lower and Middle Mississippi Valley, Lower Ohio Valley, western and central
