          <h2>Valid Friday November 20, 2026 to Thursday November 26, 2026</h2>US Hazards Outlook<br>NWS Climate Prediction Center College Park MD<br>300 PM 
EST November 12 2026<br><br><u>Synopsis</u>: Mid-level low pressure is forecast 
to extend from the Aleutians southeastward to the West Coast during mid to late 
November. An active pattern is likely across the southwestern and central 
Contiguous U.S. (CONUS) as multiple low pressure systems track eastward from 
Nov 20-26. The enhanced Pacific flow favors above-normal temperatures for a 
majority of the CONUS. During week-2, portions of the Southern Plains, however, 
may receive their first freeze this season. <br><br><u>Hazards</u> 
<ul><li>Moderate risk of heavy precipitation for eastern portions of the 
Southern Plains and the Lower Mississippi Valley, Sat-Sun, Nov 21-22.<li>Slight risk of heavy precipitation for much of the eastern Plains and 
Mississippi Valley, southwestern Great Lakes region, Lower Ohio Valley,
