          <h2>Valid Sunday November 15, 2026 to Saturday November 21, 2026</h2>US Hazards Outlook<br>NWS Climate Prediction Center College Park MD<br>300 PM 
EST November 07 2026<br><br><u>Synopsis</u>: Dynamical models continue to 
depict mid-level low pressure near the West Coast of the Contiguous U.S. 
(CONUS) leading to a potent surge of moisture into the West Coast around the 
outset of the period, particularly into portions of the Pacific Northwest and 
northern and central California. This feature is favored to weaken while 
shifting inland, but it may result in enhanced precipitation across the 
Southwest and Four Corners which could exacerbate impacts over areas recently 
impacted by heavy rainfall and flooding. Above-normal temperatures are favored 
for much of the CONUS, although areas of the east-central CONUS, Southeast, and 
Mid-Atlantic Coastal Plain may experience first frosts or freezes, which would
